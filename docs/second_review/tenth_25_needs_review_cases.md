## What this pass teaches

- We should broaden the single-survey death-label rule beyond high-health drops. L2/222 shows `alive health 2 -> dead health 0 -> alive health 1/6`, which is probably the same correction class.
- Dead-tree DBH increases often trace back to one isolated low measurement in the previous survey. The suspicious case is not just "dead DBH increased"; it is "dead-tree DBH moved away from and then returned to a baseline."
- `cluster` notes should become measurement/stem context. H0/274 is not just a DBH regime shift; it is a cluster/multi-stem measurement problem.
- A new qualifying stem that appears and persists across multiple later surveys, like H1/242, should often be a blank-history/watch case rather than urgent human review.
- Latest-survey revivals remain important. Several October 2025 alive rows appear after long dead sequences and should stay in human/source review until Riley/Juan can confirm live tissue.

## Suggested rule changes

- Add a `single_survey_low_health_death_label_outlier` route for observed-alive low health followed by one dead/0 row and then observed-alive evidence.
- Add `cluster`/`clump`/`multiple stems` note flags to measurement/stem context.
- Add a dead-tree DBH shape rule for isolated low/high remeasurement within an otherwise stable dead-tree baseline.
- Downgrade persistent new secondary stems to blank-history/watch when they recur in at least two later surveys with compatible DBHs and no identity/taxon conflict.
